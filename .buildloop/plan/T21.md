# T21: wild courses (corkscrews, hard turns, roller hills, new jumps) + T2.3 mixer drum

## Intent (user, 2026-10-08)
"implement T2.3 and also add some jumps and corkscrews and wackier courses like hard turns and stuff go wild!"

## Spec (machine-checkable acceptance)
1. T2.3: the chase-cam mixer drum turns at a rate tied to `v` (still at rest, faster at speed);
   the drum is JS-driven (`.drum`, `drumAngle`), not the fixed 2.2 s CSS spin. Reduced motion: still.
2. Ramps carry their own launch: `{x, w, h, kick, tail}`. `rampLaunch(rp, v)` is the one launch rule
   (default ramp keeps h 95 / kick 0.9, so old ramps fly exactly as before). The fairness bot calls it.
3. New ramp beats: `megaRamp` (h 150, kick 1.1, w 340, its own star arc) and `hopChain`
   (three 45-high kickers, a whee each). Flight zones computed from physics (`rampTail`), nothing
   blocks a landing.
4. Corkscrews: `TWISTS [{x0, x1, dir}]`, `rollAt(x)` 0..2pi eased. The road ahead rolls around the
   view axis by `rollAt(x) - rollAt(car)`, ground + sky + roadside by `-rollAt(car)`, car stays
   upright. Hoops ring the twist. No blockers inside a twist (stars only, a helix trail). Reduced
   motion: no roll, hoops only.
5. Hard turns: `CURVE.hard`, course shapes `switchback` (alternating hard hairpins) and `zigzag`
   (sharp kinks), red/white chevron boards on the outside of every hard turn.
6. `roller`: a train of big short hills regardless of the world's hill setting.
7. `corkscrew` course shape; every world's ten-level order gets two or three of the new shapes;
   finales from world 2 add a twist.
8. Fairness bots pass all 120 levels unchanged thresholds; all 22 suites + lint green.
9. New suite checks: course-check (twists, roll math, hard-turn chevrons, ramp kinds, drum).
